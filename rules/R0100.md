Update the loose-end tracker at its checkpoints, rather than waiting for Dave to remember it:

- flush the session's open items into it before the session ends;
- account for every item in it — resolved, deferred with a rationale, or accepted risk — before a release gate;
- land every scan finding you do not resolve on the spot in it before the delta closes;
- produce its current state on demand, and update the file as you do;
- surface an item from it as soon as it becomes relevant to the work at hand.
