Raise a risk as a finding and leave the weighing to the release decision: reserve `blocking` for a gap the governing policies forbid releasing with, and distinguish material risk from acceptable risk in what you raise. Not every gap needs automation, not every live test belongs in the fast run, and a theoretical concern does not stop progress.

## Human

Blocking by default: likely user-visible breakage, missing auth or config verification for a critical path, security or privacy exposure, data loss risk, unrecoverable operational failure, no evidence for central acceptance criteria, degraded UX under some conditions, known dependency risk, and shipping with fallback behavior. Deferred by default: non-critical live smoke automation, broader device or browser coverage, additional synthetic monitoring, and fixture refresh work.
