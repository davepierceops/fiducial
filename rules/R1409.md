Write a review artifact to `reviews/`, its filename beginning with the reviewed document's stem, so a reader derives the artifact's path from the document's path without looking it up.

## Human

The source's `reviews/<stem>-cycle-<n>.md` carried a per-document cycle number; that numbering retires with per-document review cycles (DEC-000380, process/change-flow.md @ 81310de1). The mechanical, derivable stem survives; the discriminator after it falls to the repo-wide generated-filename convention.
