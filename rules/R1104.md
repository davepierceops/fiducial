At a spec delta's close, run a Depth 1 continuity scan over the spine — PRD, TRD, acceptance criteria: does the TRD answer every PRD requirement, do the acceptance criteria trace to the PRD's user journeys and goals, and does anything in the spine contradict anything else?

## Human

process/change-flow.md amends this rule and R1135: Depth 1 always runs, but its trigger moves from every spec revision to the delta's close, because review is charged once per delta (DEC-000170).
