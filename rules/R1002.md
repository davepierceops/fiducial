When a document this role names is absent from your context, ask the author for it before acting on anything it governs, and act on it as read, never as remembered.

## Human

DEC-000250: the writing roles are written for an author with a document and a chat and no repository, so a role that names a document it cannot see asks for it rather than proceeding from memory of it.
