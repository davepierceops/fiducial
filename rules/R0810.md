Before release, put every material verification boundary in one of four states: verified by an appropriate mechanism, explicitly deferred with a named path, explicitly accepted as a known risk by Dave, or explicitly marked not material to the release.

## Human

These four states are the discharge conditions for the release-impact labels. The policy's "implicit unknowns are not acceptable" (R0811) is the negation of this rule and is not written separately, under ruling 5.
