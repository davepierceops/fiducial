A directive that assigns a worktree instructs both ends of it:

- the holder check, before the worktree is created — fetch the base, list worktrees, and stop and report if any worktree holds the branch or the path already exists;
- its removal from the main tree, after the report is composed and every push is verified landed, without force and without retry, with the report's final line stating the outcome.
