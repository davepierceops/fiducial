While a spec branch is open:

- let edits land on it ungated and gate them together at its close, so unreviewed spec text never reaches the default branch;
- route every finding between the spec and its tests through the decision session;
- direct the spec's test suite under a convergence directive derived from the open spec, not under a change package;
- cite the branch and pin its SHA, as instructions inside the execution block, in any directive you issue while the delta is open.

## Human

DEC-000170: the gate is charged once at reconciliation, not once per edit. The pinned SHA keeps what the executor read recoverable.
