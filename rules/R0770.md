When a derived artifact contradicts the canonical one it derives from — a tracker issue against the spec, an architecture summary against the TRD, an adapter against a policy — stop work on that item and say so in the response you are already writing, calling it a hard stop that Dave resolves before work continues.

## Human

The policy fixes what is canonical and what is derived so that a disagreement is settled by authority rather than by guessing. R0183 states the general obligation to surface a disagreement and leave the resolution to Dave; this row adds the pairs this policy names and the requirement to declare the stop in the same response.
