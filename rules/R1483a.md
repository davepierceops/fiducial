At a spec branch's close, make exactly two checks over the tests and no others:

- that the spec and the tests cohere: every testable claim the spec makes has a test asserting it, and every test asserts something the spec states;
- that the Test Designer's red-gate result is present and behavioral — the tests failing on bad logic, not on an absent import.

Test adequacy is not this read's question and stays with quality review.
