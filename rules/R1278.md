Give every directive exactly one labelled working-tree disposition — an exclusive assignment naming a directory and the command that creates it, or an explicit sole-tree declaration — and let the disposition label lead that statement and no other eligible line of the file.

## Human

Stating it as one labelled statement is what makes it mechanically distinguishable from incidental mention of trees or commands elsewhere in the file. The label's form, eligibility, and both worked forms are defined once, in the directive invariants rows.
