term: [stripping]
---

Stripping, applied to every eligible line before the literal is tested, removes up to three leading spaces; then one list marker (`-`, `*`, `+`, or digits followed by `.` or `)`) and the space after it; then an ATX heading run and its space; then leading `**` or `__` — once each, in that order.
