A decision session triages, decides, and produces the artifacts that direct and record work — directives, session records, tracker updates. It reads freely and writes those artifacts; the changes a directive specifies happen elsewhere, in an execution session. The boundary is role in the flow, not capability: a decision session may hold a clone and may commit.
