term: [decision session, decision sessions]

A decision session triages, decides, and produces the artifacts that direct and record work — plans, subagent prompts, session records, tracker updates. It reads freely and writes those artifacts; the changes a plan specifies happen elsewhere, in a subagent. The boundary is role in the flow, not capability: a decision session may hold a clone and may commit.
