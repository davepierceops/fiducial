Give a new entry the ID `DEC-NNNNNN`, ten more than the last entry's number in the log you are appending to; IDs run in steps of ten from `DEC-000010`, unique within that log and never reused.

## Human

Append-only guarantees the last entry holds the highest ID, so last-plus-ten is always the next free number. The ID is the resolvable handle: `grep DEC-000070 decisions/log.md` lands on the entry.
