A block renders copyable whole in the surface that delivers it, with that surface's copy control intact; avoid constructs known to break the surface in use.

## Human

A block the reader cannot copy whole is not a paste block at all. The failure is invisible from the author's side: the text is well-formed and every command valid, and the problem appears only in rendering, which the author does not see.
