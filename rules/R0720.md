Judge what a remote write landed from the repository's own log read against the fetched remote — not from the tool's response, and not from your recollection of what you sent.

## Human

The failure this guards against is that a response is a claim about a write rather than evidence of one. The rule holds under any version control system: the repository's own log outranks the tool's response.
