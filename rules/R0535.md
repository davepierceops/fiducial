A change is consequential if it affects authentication or authorization; schema or data migrations; security, privacy, or user-data visibility or sharing; irreversible or hard-to-reverse operations; first user exposure of a feature or surface; breaking changes to public interfaces; pricing, billing, or entitlements; verification boundaries (live integrations, external-data fixtures, or verification classes); core architecture in the spec; an architecture summary that moves a boundary; or a Top K journey with 20% or less SLO error budget remaining. Other changes are routine and may release on evidence without explicit human approval.
