Review a change in one pass over the whole of it — all files, the test plan, the diff and the boundary updates together — never file by file.

## Human

This is the quality pass: it covers the whole change at once, carries its own verdict line, and is never merged with the Skeptic/Risk pass, which asks its own question over the same diff (process/change-flow.md). Every pull request gets this review by an agent, with no trivial-change exemption (process/change-flow.md).

In force until an adopted, published code-review standard replaces it: process/change-flow.md's Quality bullet holds the Reviewer role's rows in force until that standard lands through intake, and the intake commit that lands it retires them.
