Where two entries have landed on the same number, renumber the later ones by plus one within the gap — `DEC-000410` to `DEC-000411`, `DEC-000412` — not by another ten.

## Human

The step of ten exists for exactly this: there is always room to bump a collision without disturbing its neighbours.
