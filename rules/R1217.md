Put no `exit`, `exec`, `logout`, `|| { …; exit; }`, or `set -e` in a command block. Each ends the interactive shell it runs in — `set -e` on the next failing command, exactly as `exit` does, while being the idiomatic opening line of a careful multi-command block. They are known instances of the shell-terminating rule, not its extent.
