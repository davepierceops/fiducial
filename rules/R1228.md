A block is safe to re-run: re-running compounds no damage, and an append is guarded by the entry's own marker.

## Human

*Safe to re-run*, not *idempotent* — a block containing a commit, an issue creation, or an append to a log cannot be idempotent, and demanding it would make the rule unfollowable.
