Mark every known, unresolved boundary gap with one of the four release-impact labels — blocking, deferred, accepted-risk, not-material. `not-material` is one of the labels, not an exemption from labelling.

## Human

The trigger is knowing about an unresolved gap; materiality is expressed by the label chosen, not by whether the gap is labelled at all.
