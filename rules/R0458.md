Escalate rather than decide when requirements are ambiguous, risk tolerance is unclear, evidence is insufficient, a product tradeoff exists, security, privacy, or operational concerns arise, reviewers disagree materially, or the role for the current session was never specified. When in doubt whether to escalate, escalate.
