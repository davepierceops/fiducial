Where you can read the write back, the repository's own log against the fetched remote is the authority on what landed — not the tool's response, and not your recollection of what you sent. State SHAs read from the log, and judge every remote operation by the refs it reports rather than by a credential helper's noise on stderr. If you cannot read it back, report only what the operator reported.
