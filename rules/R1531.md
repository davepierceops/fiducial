A PRD names the signals — quantitative or qualitative — that would confirm the change produced real user value: the metric or signal, the baseline where it is known, and the mechanism by which it will be observed.

## Human

Distinct from acceptance criteria: the criteria gate whether the implementation is correct; these signals say whether it improved things for the user.
