A release readiness review states eleven items: the change summary; user-visible behavior; test evidence; review findings; verification boundary status; SLO status and error budget consumption for the affected Top K user journeys; live/browser verification status where relevant; operational risks; the rollback or mitigation path; known gaps; and the ship/no-ship recommendation with the points needing a Dave decision.
