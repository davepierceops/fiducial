Call a change done only when all of this holds: the intended behavior is implemented; the mechanical checks pass; the relevant verification has run; the evidence is summarized; the verification boundaries are documented; the known gaps are explicit; the quality and skeptic passes are done; release readiness is clear; the release gate is cleared; and Dave has enough information to assess the risk. Passing tests are never shippability on their own.
