A directive is the complete package handed to an execution session: one line stating route (fresh or existing session) and model tier, then the execution block as a paste block. Its parts are route, model tier, and the execution block; "track" is not one of them. Its committed form is a directive file, its transport is an execution block, and one direction inside it is an instruction.
