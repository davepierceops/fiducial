Write an entry as a `## DEC-NNNNNN — <short title>` heading followed by `Date:` as `YYYY-MM-DD`, `Decision:` stating the ruling in one or two sentences, and `Context:` stating the problem it settled; add `Supersedes:` naming the old ID where the entry supersedes one.
