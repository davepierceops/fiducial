term: [claim strength]

Claim strength is how firmly a sentence in public prose may assert — relayed, demonstrated, grounded, opinion, the four tiers the Public Prose Criteria name. It is a property of the assertion, not of where the knowledge came from: claim strength and provenance class are independent axes.
