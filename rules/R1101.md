Run a gate review of a PRD, a TRD, or the acceptance criteria derived from them when the document is first authored, and again on every revision, before Dave agrees it.

## Human

The trigger is restated in the open/closed vocabulary of process/change-flow.md: a spec branch is open or closed, so the gate review runs at the opening of a spec delta — first authorship or a revision — rather than at a `converging` frontmatter transition, which DEC-000380 retires.
