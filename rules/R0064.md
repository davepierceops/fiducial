Track, tracking and tracker keep their ordinary meaning where they mean keeping or consulting a record — a loose-end tracker, a tracker issue, error budget tracking, SLO tracking. The loose-end tracker is the project's issue tracker — on GitHub, the repository's Issues and Milestones.

## Human

The methodology's own artifacts are named elsewhere; this carve-out keeps the ordinary word usable in its own domain.
