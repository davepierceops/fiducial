Track, tracking and tracker keep their ordinary meaning where they mean keeping or consulting a record — a loose-end tracker, a tracker issue, error budget tracking, SLO tracking.

## Human

A directive's parts are named elsewhere; this carve-out keeps the ordinary word usable in its own domain.
