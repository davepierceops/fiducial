Run quality and skepticism as separate passes, each with its own verdict, and never merge them into one pass; the class of the delta decides whether the two passes run in one session or two.

## Human

Quality asks "is this good?"; skepticism asks "where is this lying to us?" — a change can pass one and fail the other. process/change-flow.md makes each dimension a pass with its own verdict line and lets the delta's class, not the dimensions, decide the number of sessions.
