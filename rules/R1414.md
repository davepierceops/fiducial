Carry one overall `Verdict:` line in a review artifact's header and one `Verdict (<pass>):` line at the head of each pass section; every verdict is `ready`, `ready-with-findings`, or `changes-required`, and the overall verdict is the most severe of the pass verdicts on that order. `ready` means ready for Dave's agreement, never that the document is agreed.
