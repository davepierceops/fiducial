Carry one overall `Verdict:` line in the header, and open each pass section with its own `Verdict (<pass>):` line — continuity, quality, skepticism, and any further dimension in force. Every verdict is `ready`, `ready-with-findings`, or `changes-required`, and the overall verdict is the most severe of the pass verdicts on that order. `ready` means ready for Dave's agreement.
