Surface suspected drift between a canonical artifact and one derived from it when you meet it; do not wait for a scan.

## Human

Drift is caught proactively so it never reaches the hard stop. The Spec Reviewer's continuity scans are the designated mechanism for that, not the only one — anyone who meets drift raises it.
