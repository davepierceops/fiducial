A block carries no construct that can end the shell it is pasted into — `exit`, `exec`, `logout`, `|| { …; exit; }`, and `set -e` are known instances, not the extent of the rule; `set -e` ends the shell on the next failing command exactly as `exit` does, while being the idiomatic opening line of a careful multi-command block.

## Human

The rule is stated by effect, not by a list of constructs. On most terminals, ending an interactive shell closes its window.
