The skepticism pass is a stage of the change flow with gate force: it is a pass of its own, with its own verdict line, never merged with the quality pass. The Critic is a different role — advisory to Dave, never a gate.

## Human

process/change-flow.md makes skepticism one of three review dimensions and holds that quality and skepticism are never merged into one pass: a change can pass one and fail the other, so the second question is asked after the first has been answered.
