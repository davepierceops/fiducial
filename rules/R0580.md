Resolve a decision to the decision log: cite it by its DEC ID, so a reader finds it by searching that ID in the log.

## Human

So a decision made once can be found and honored — not silently re-litigated or contradicted because no one knew it existed. A cited decision with no defined path back to its definition is the defect this log removes.
