The decision log is the one place a decision resolves to: a decision cited by its DEC ID is found by searching that ID in the log.

## Human

So a decision made once can be found and honored — not silently re-litigated or contradicted because no one knew it existed. A cited decision with no defined path back to its definition is the defect this log removes (R0587, merged here). DEC-000380 keeps the log and makes it the record for prose documents gated by a frontier read.
