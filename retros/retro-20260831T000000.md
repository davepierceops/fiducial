# Retro — fiducial — 2026-08-28

## Context

One decision session carried the directive-tooling PRD from a stalled cycle-22
fix through agreement, then authored, gated, converged, and jointly flipped its
TRD with a 149-test red-gated suite under a process Dave described in chat and
ruled to run ad hoc before canonizing. Roughly the first third of the session
was spent on a dispatch stall (four executor stops on one directive) whose
cause turned out to be session-local, not environmental. The remainder ran
eleven executor sessions and six connector merges with no stop that was not a
correct stop.

   sandbox-wide deny on `.git/worktrees`. Dave: "I just don't believe you

## Interpretation

- **Executor self-reports about their environment are told-class claims, and
  a stuck session can be confidently wrong** (1, 2). The stall was resolved by
  a probe that depended on trusting neither party, not by more reasoning.
- **Baton-carried "observations" are told, not observed** (3). A false
  observation in a baton propagated into a ruling; reading the file once
  corrected it. Cheap to check, expensive to inherit.
- **The connector's real failure surface is full-file rebuilds** (5, 6). Every
  small-diff connector operation this session was fine; the two dangerous
  moments were both whole-file regeneration of a large document. Edit-in-place
  by an executor plus stats verification removes the surface entirely.
- **Own-worktree cleanup is the fix for the cross-session lock** (8). Whatever
  the mechanism, the rule "remove your own tree, report it as your last line"
  eliminated the failure class for the rest of the session.
- **The convergence process works, and its cost is one extra confirmation pass
  per amendment round** (11, 14). The second confirmation caught exactly the
  kind of defect an amendment introduces — stranded cross-references and
  unrevised test metadata — which no author of the amendment would see.
  Round-trip cost was small; skipping it would have flipped a TRD with a wrong
  row number.
- **Tests find what reading cannot** (13). Three mechanism defects in an agreed-
  by-reading TRD were caught only because the Test Designer ran git. This is
  the strongest argument for tests as co-equal reviewer rather than downstream
  consumer.
- **Dispositions are intent; executors verify them against the counterparty
  artifact** (12). A dictated wording that contradicts the tests is an error in
  the disposition, and the mediation loop should expect and welcome that
  correction rather than demand literal compliance.
- **Pre-staging means emitted** (9). A dispatch of already-ruled work is not a
  judgment call and must not be gated on a y/n.

## Durable insights

- When a session and the environment disagree, run a one-line diagnostic in a
  fresh session before either debugging or believing.
- Verify inherited observations by reading the file before encoding them into
  a ruling. A baton's "observed" is the next session's "told".
- Never regenerate a large governed file through the connector. Small verified
  diffs over the connector; in-place edits by executors for anything else.
- Every directive: own-worktree removal as the last act, status as the report's
  last line; holder check before creation; "if gh fails, skip the PR and say
  so."
- The spec/tests convergence process: one blocker-scoped gate, spec stays
  open, Test Designer files findings and never edits the spec, decision session
  mediates dispositions, a confirmation pass after each amendment round, joint
  flip on a clean confirmation. Ran clean end-to-end; ready to canonize.
- Read PR state before retrying a timed-out merge.

## Candidate methodology changes

- **operating-model.md change flow**: add the spec/tests convergence variant
  as the standing shape for TRDs that will have a test suite — steps 1 and 4
  interleave; agreement is joint. Inputs: the directive-tooling-trd cycle
  directives and reviews/directive-tooling-trd-cycle-{1,2,3}.md.
- **skills/directive-authoring.md**: (a) every directive ends with own-worktree
  removal and reports its status as the final line; (b) holder check before
  worktree creation; (c) a PR-opening instruction carries the gh-failure
  fallback; (d) dispositions are intent — executors verify against the
  counterparty artifact and disclose deviations rather than comply literally.
- **decision-layer.md rule 5 (pre-stage)**: state that dispatch of ruled work
  is emitted, not offered.
- **specs/prd-template.md, specs/trd-template.md**: audience `[human]` — a
  directed fix, ruled 2026-08-28, not a candidate.
- **A connector-write policy line** (commit-and-change-control or a skill):
  connector writes are for small diffs verified by stats and read-back; whole-
  file regeneration of governed documents is not a connector operation.
- **Core rule 8 or the baton skill**: an observation carried across sessions
  is re-labelled told on receipt.
