## Interpretation

- The re-read-before-authoring rule is load-bearing under concurrent expedited edits (5): without it, two dispatches would have been authored against superseded governing text.
- Carried claims are the weakest link in the provenance chain (3, 7): two incidents in one session, both caught downstream by readers who checked. The label-carried-claims rule targets exactly this.
- Observed-over-carried evidence is finding real defects that reading misses (4, 9): reviewer re-probing twice exceeded the written record. The scratch-substrate permission converts a rule collision into standard practice.
- Fix seams regenerate defects (8): both sweep-caught defects were created by the cycle's own edits. A directed sweep naming the seam is cheap and caught both; the cycle-3 reviewer's column-rule diagnosis (O2) suggests rule-level fixes close seams that cell-level fixes keep reopening.
- The cycle split delivered its promised decision-record hygiene (one directive per cycle conversation, decisions in one place) at a real relay cost to Dave (10). The trade held for a three-cycle series; whether it holds at higher cycle counts or for smaller documents is unmeasured.
- Pointers-not-prescriptions appears to be working: zero moved gaps this series (12) against one known instance under verbatim prescription in the PRD series.
- Stats verification went six-for-six with no catches (2); the historical 19-byte incident still justifies its cost.

## Durable insights

- Re-read governing skills immediately before authoring, every time — mid-session drift in governed text is a live condition, not a theoretical one.
- Label every claim a directive carries from a session report with its provenance class; flat-fact carriage was falsified once and near-missed once in one session.
- Scope worktree dispositions to repository work and permit scratch substrates for evidence-gathering; probes in throwaway repos are where assertions go to die.
- Every fix gets a directed consistency sweep naming its seam; expect the sweep to catch the cycle's own edits, not just history.
- Series-stemmed names (`<doc>-<n>-<timestamp>Z`) survive concurrent multi-document cycling; bare numbered forms collide.
