# Retro — fiducial — 2026-09-05

## Context

Session 8 opened on a baton whose ordered work was a tracker flush, then a TRD for the bundle-system PRD. The flush landed. The TRD was drafted, presented, and then abandoned within the hour when Dave, prompted by the draft's selection mechanism, questioned the file-as-bag-of-rules model itself and ruled a rebuild: rules become rows with free key-value pairs, gates move from documents to content, the lifecycle machinery retires, the name and history stay. Two frontier-tier passes then extracted 1600 rows from the 54 agent-facing files and clustered them to 1131 distinct rules. A shape document and a PRD for the rebuild were drafted in the pane. Three PRs merged. Baton emitted before this retro.

## Evidence

Dave's next message did not engage with it; he asked whether to "just move to the model where there's just a database of instructions with tags." The draft's most worked-out section was the one that surfaced the larger question.

Dave replied that it was a git-mechanics question he does not care about, answered the naming question instead, and delegated the mechanics with two constraints.
4. Dave asked for the word "key-value" and could not retrieve it; the CoS supplied it in one line and connected it to what the frontmatter already is. Dave then proposed 0-to-N free pairs per row unprompted, and later asked whether an unknown number of pairs was a complexity worry. The plain answer (a dictionary; two typed keys; every value a list of words) settled it.
5. Dave noticed, unprompted, that files did a grouping job the row model had not replaced, and asked for a "what is this about" key. The CoS proposed seeding `topic` from the source path rather than designing a vocabulary.
6. Dave dictated the storage-boundary rule in his own words as an instruction; the CoS put it in verbatim; Dave then asked for the rationale to be added, saying he had over-worded it, and asked for it to "sound better." Four sentences were accepted.
7. The CoS wrote "I'll change the shape to say exactly that" and did not. Dave asked "Did you though?" The change was made after the question.
8. Two edits to a presented file did not appear in the pane. The pane keys on filename; re-presenting the same path did not refresh; a fresh timestamped copy did. A two-file present showed only the first file.
9. Pass 3 extraction: 54 files, 1600 rows, 15/15 sampled sources verified, in 29 minutes. Pass 3 clustering: 839 mechanical pairs, 218 sweep pairs with zero shared words all adjudicated same, 1131 distinct rules, arithmetic verified by command, in 26 minutes. Both executors reported their own mid-run errors (an id-numbering bug; a 32-row over-merge) and the corrections.
10. Three `bin/check-directive` M2 false positives fired on this session's directives: "path:lines @ sha", "each @ sha", and a companion cited at main's SHA rather than the commit that touched the file. All three were resolved by rewording, not by changing the tool.
11. The August dedup (Pass 2) compared 220 of 878 rows and was never used. The September pass was designed with a mechanical shortlist plus a mandatory per-bucket sweep, and the executor's report says the sweep found what no threshold could.
12. Rotation rule CS-5 was honoured: the CoS proposed continuing before the TRD, was acked; proposed handoff before the Store package, and Dave said "baton."

## Interpretation

- (1, 2) The pre-staged artifact did its job in an unexpected way: a fully worked wrong answer is what made the right question askable. The TRD was not wasted; it was the evidence that the model needed changing. But the CoS's instinct on the follow-up was conservative — recommend the option that keeps the most existing machinery — and Dave's was not. The recommendation should have been weighed against "what would we build if we started today," not against "what carries over."
- (3) The decision-layer rule "ask the judgment calls" was applied to a question that was not Dave's. Mechanics are the CoS's; the question he needed was naming, and he answered that one. The tell was that the CoS could answer its own question once Dave declined it.
- (4, 5, 6) When Dave reasons in plain terms about data structures he arrives at the right shape quickly; what he needs from the CoS is the word, the ordinary name for the thing, and a sentence connecting it to something already in the repository. He also does the grouping and rationale work himself once the structure is clear. The CoS's job in those exchanges was vocabulary and confirmation, not design.
- (7) A stated intention to edit is a claim about a future write. The write must precede the claim, or the claim is "I will" and not "done." This is Core rule 12 (a tool's success response is a claim) applied to the CoS's own prose.
- (8) The pane is an operator; its success is not observable from the CoS's side. Fresh filenames per edit and one file per present are the workaround; a state-read after present would be the fix if the surface offered one.
- (9, 11) Two frontier passes in under an hour did what the August passes could not, because the directive stated the method (shortlist, then sweep, then verify by re-reading source) rather than the goal. The sweep finding 218 zero-overlap restatements is the strongest evidence yet that lexical dedup alone is insufficient for this corpus and that a judgment pass must be budgeted as a first-class step.
- (10) M2's false positives are now a pattern with three known trigger phrasings. Rewording works every time; the cost is a lint cycle per directive.

## Durable insights

- **Recommend from a blank page, then price the carry-over.** When a model change is on the table, the first recommendation should be the design that would be built today; what existing machinery survives is a second question, priced separately. Recommending the option that preserves the most is a bias, not a judgment.
- **The CoS answers its own mechanics questions.** If a question can be answered by the CoS once Dave declines it, it was never his; ask the naming or judgment question underneath it instead.
- **Supply the ordinary word.** When Dave reaches for a term, give the common name and one sentence tying it to something already in the tree. He does the rest.
- **Do the edit before saying it is done.** "I'll change X" is a defect unless X is already changed in the same turn.
- **Method in the directive, not goal.** Extraction and dedup passes succeed when the directive states the steps (mechanical shortlist, per-bucket sweep, source re-read on the largest clusters, arithmetic by command) and fail when they state only the outcome.
- **Lexical dedup is a shortlist, not a check.** At this corpus's paraphrase density, a judgment sweep found more true duplicates than the mechanical shortlist did; budget it every time.
- **Pane discipline:** fresh timestamped filename after every edit; one file per present.

## Candidate methodology changes

- **Decision Layer, rule 6 ("Ask the judgment calls"):** add — a question the CoS could answer itself once declined was not a judgment call; ask the question beneath it. Input to the process/ document that replaces the Decision Layer under the rebuild.
- **Core, rule 12 extension or new row:** a stated intention to write an artifact is not a record of it; the write precedes the statement in the same turn. Input for intake.
- **Directive-authoring skill (or its successor row):** dedup and extraction directives state the method — shortlist thresholds, sweep by bucket, verification by source re-read and by command — and the mandatory sweep is named as a step, not an option. Also: the next shortlist run uses ratio ≥ 0.70 or Jaccard ≥ 0.10 as a floor, per the executor's tuning note.
- **check-directive M2:** three false-positive phrasings are now classified; either the lint learns "@ sha, lines N–M" and "read from the base ref <sha>", or the directive-authoring guidance names the phrasings to avoid. Input for the Tool package, since the lint survives the rebuild.
- **Rule-clusters artifact:** allow a compound row to appear in two clusters (as Pass 2 did) rather than the strict partition, or require compound rows split at extraction. Input for the Store package's directive.
