## Durable insights

- A deletion or move package is directed by outcome and acceptance criteria, with decision rights over what the spec's goals and non-goals already settle; enumerating paths from a partial read guarantees a stop per miss.
- An execution session with a clone can settle in one run what the decision session cannot see through a connector in five; the decision session's job is the outcome, the boundaries, and the decisions the spec does not make.
- Agreement of a spec at a SHA says the human agreed the text; it says nothing about whether the ACs hold on the tree. The two need separate evidence.
- Untracked, per-clone state — installed hooks, credential helpers, `$TMPDIR` — is outside every test's reach and belongs in a boundary declaration when a package changes it.
- The verification of a verification block: a grep against a diff line must account for the line's own leading character (E13).
