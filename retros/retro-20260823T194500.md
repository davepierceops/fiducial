## Interpretation

- Restated lore has compounding costs: refusal risk when it reads as injection (1), drift when a rationale is wrong and gets recopied (4, 8), and a per-directive tax. Provenance-by-citation (2) fixed the refusal class; only encapsulation in tooling fixes the drift and the tax (3, 9).
- Executor stop conditions are load-bearing and cheap (1, 8, 10): both failures this session were caught before any state corruption, by the guard clause itself. The enforcement point for directive linting already demonstrably works (9).
- The recurring failure is structural, not behavioral (8, 9): invariant text composed freehand by a context-degraded author will keep failing at a base rate; the fix is shrinking the freehand surface and gating what remains, mirroring how every other artifact class in the methodology is already treated.
- A naive-sounding question from the owner produced a simpler design than my staged options (7): present the option space including "dissolve the distinction," not just the alternatives that survived my own first pass.
- Refusals may fire on paste position before content (5): kickoff hygiene is a distinct variable from directive content, unverified and worth testing deliberately.
- Won't-fix with no tracking (12) is a real disposition: some findings cost more to carry than to drop.

## Durable insights

- Every directive carries a provenance line for standing constraints: path @ SHA, "carry as told." Assertions without provenance read as injection to a well-calibrated executor.
- A working-tree clause is an assignment — named directory plus creation command — never a prohibition. Prohibitions don't tell the executor what to do when the guard fires.
- Prefer evidence-trail detection over mode-based prevention when the tool would otherwise have to guess intent (print the prior head; let the decision session triage).
- Structural fixes over discipline: any instruction that must be remembered per-artifact will eventually be dropped; move invariants into generated text and machine checks.
- Narrow session scope is a context-quality control, not just an organizational preference.
