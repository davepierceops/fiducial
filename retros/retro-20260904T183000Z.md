## Interpretation
- (1, 2) Read-before-asserting plus independent branch verification produced zero executor-side corrections across six directives. The cost is one sandbox clone per session; the return is that every merge was verified by two parties.
- (3, 6) Both findings that reached the fix round were decision-session defects: one from an earlier cycle (converging never scoped when introduced), one from this session (adjacent-sentence duplication in dictated wording). The reviewer gate caught both; the Editor executor caught the second before the gate did. Executors reading adjacent text is cheap insurance the dictation step lacks.
- (4, 5, 13) Dave's rulings were fastest and firmest when the question was preceded by a one-sentence definition of the term at issue and framed as "match what you ruled before, reversibly" versus "new machinery". A question stated in the corpus's own vocabulary stalled.
- (7, 12) Carrying small checks into a gate as explicit one-line items, and naming tree facts as out of scope, kept the artifact from filing non-findings and avoided a fix round for them.
- (8, 9, 10) Tooling friction was all pre-known or immediately classifiable; none delayed a merge. Recording it in the baton is working.
- (11) Stating the count and requiring per-span read-back closed the truncation failure mode from #314 on its first application.

## Durable insights
- Before asking Dave a ruling that turns on a corpus term, define the term in one sentence and say which option matches his prior rulings. Offer the reversible option explicitly.
- Before dictating a sentence, read the sentence on either side of the insertion point at the reviewed ref; adjacent restatement is a finding class.
- Every tracker or dictation directive states its item count and requires byte-for-byte read-back per span.
- A status or mechanism introduced by one cycle should carry a scope statement (which documents it reaches, who owns its exit) at introduction; the gate that introduces it should check for that explicitly.
- bin/directive: detach onto origin/main before generating; general mode needs --write and stdin redirected.
