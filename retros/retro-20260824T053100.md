# Retro — fiducial — 2026-08-24

## Context

Decision session dispatched to spec `bin/directive` (skeleton generator) and `bin/check-directive` (executor-run lint) — the countermeasure to directives being the only ungated load-bearing artifact class. The session ran 19 authoring cycles and 3 independent gates over `specs/directive-tooling.md`, landed 5 expedited amendments to `skills/directive-authoring.md`, and ended with a Dave-agreed bounded endgame (one confirmation-scoped gate, then agreement) handed to a successor session via baton.

2. `push -u` from a `$TMPDIR` worktree failed only its config write (upstream tracking targets the shared clone's config); pushing without `-u` worked every time.

6. Cycles 10, 11 and 17 each produced a blocker created by that same cycle's dictated fix; the cycle-11 executor named the streak explicitly.
7. Five expedited amendments to `skills/directive-authoring.md` landed in one day (mandatory disposition; two naming forms; timestamp components; labelled statement; exactly-one), each a small diff, each triggered by a spec finding that the lint would otherwise enforce ungoverned text (G6).

## Interpretation

- Author-and-gate in one session passes defects that fresh eyes catch, in both directions: independent gates caught what self-gates passed (4), and one self-gate caught what independent gates missed (5). Neither replaces the other; alternation is the working structure.
- A dictated fix is authored text and carries the freehand error rate the spec exists to fix; the fix-creates-next-blocker streak (6) plus the CoS's own citation and role-line defects (3, 8) are the degraded-author thesis demonstrating on its own project. The countermeasure that ended the streak was structural each time: governed text (7), descope (12), or a ruling (12) — never more careful wording.
- Convergence stalls are diagnosable by finding-class, not by count (11, 12): when blockers become mechanism-grade detail below the document's level, the document is carrying claims from the wrong stage, and descoping — not fixing — is the move.
- The convergence criterion must be stated before the loop starts (11); an unstated criterion drifts toward zero-findings, which added text makes unreachable.
- Small expedited amendments are an effective release valve for governed-text gaps found mid-spec (7), but five in a day on one file is a consolidation debt signal, and the eligibility flag re-surfacing on every one (executors flagged condition 3 five times) suggests the ruling should be recorded once, somewhere citable.
- Executor stop discipline paid for itself every time it fired (1, 9): the cost was one relay round-trip; the avoided cost was a wrong tree or a corrupt origin-of-record.
- Duplicate-paste detection by SHA (10) is a cheap, reliable triage guard in a multi-session workflow where the human is the relay.

## Durable insights

- **Alternate self-gates with independent gates on long-lived documents; bind the final gate's scope explicitly** (confirmation of named resolutions, not fresh full-depth) or the loop never terminates.
- **State the convergence criterion at loop start**: which verdicts permit agreement, and who weighs residual non-blockings. The policy already said ready-with-findings suffices; the session forgot it for ten cycles.
- **When consecutive blockers are below the document's abstraction level, descope rather than fix** — route the mechanism to the next stage's question list.
- **A recurring finding that the lint/reviewer "enforces ungoverned text" is a governed-text gap, not a spec defect**: amend the small governed sentence, then re-anchor.
- **A cross-document contradiction surfaced by a spec is Dave's Core-rule-9 ruling, not the spec's problem to absorb**; three cycles were spent absorbing one before it was named as such.
- **Statement vs mention (unfenced vs fenced) is a load-bearing distinction** for any mechanical check over prose that quotes its own subject matter.
- **Sandbox lore accreted this session, all generator-encapsulation candidates**: `$TMPDIR` worktrees only; push without `-u`; ROLES line mandatory; provenance by commit SHA never blob; exactly one labelled disposition statement.
- **Directive-then-baton ordering**: with context loaded, the directive is cheap and the baton can point at it; emitted the other way, the baton must restate what the directive would have carried.

## Candidate methodology changes

- **Consolidation cycle for `skills/directive-authoring.md`** (full review, not expedited): merge five same-day amendments into coherent prose; record the condition-3 eligibility ruling for this document so executors stop re-flagging it. (Overdue; already in the baton.)
- **Add to the spec-review or operating-model text**: a convergence clause — before a multi-cycle review effort starts, the decision session states the agreement bar and the independent-gate cadence; a gate may be scope-bound to confirmation.
- **Add to `skills/directive-authoring.md` or the generator's invariant set**: every directive states its ROLES; fix-cycle directives are not exempt.
- **Review-artifact or reviewer-role addendum**: findings below the reviewed document's stage level (TRD-grade detail in a PRD) are routed as stage questions, not filed as blockers — candidate wording for the Spec Reviewer role.
- **Command-blocks or lore home**: fold the session's sandbox lore into committed text the generator reads, retiring the per-directive carry (this is precisely `bin/directive`'s job; the change is to stop hand-carrying once it ships).
