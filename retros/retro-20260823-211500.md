# Retro — fiducial — 2026-08-23

## Context

Chief of Staff decision session managing the bin/land spec through its review
pipeline: triaged the cycle-2 execution report, ran cycles 3–7 (directive →
execution → re-gate → merge each), closed all four open questions, landed the
OPEN-ITEMS gate entry, and executed the agreement flip via bin/flip-agreed.
specs/bin-land.md reached agreed at flip commit 3a8e55e3, merged at 930c4958.

## Evidence

1. Cycles 3, 4, and 5 each produced new non-blocking findings that were
   artifacts of the previous cycle's fixes: cycle-3's verbatim-prescribed
   clause for AC-LAND-01b narrowed its given off the common case (cycle-4 F1);
   cycle-4's fix left the identical defect in sibling 01a (cycle-5 F1).
2. Cycle-6's reviewer observed that the applied Fix text removed "whose
   checked-out branch is" — a coverage change (detached-HEAD trees) larger
   than the Fix field's own "Two words." claim, and surfaced the discrepancy
   against the review record rather than the spec.
3. Cycle-5's reviewer found cycle-4's Evidence field asserted, under an
   *observed* label, a claim false against the SHA it reviewed; it surfaced
   the disagreement and did not retrofit the artifact.
4. The bin-land-agree executor's worktree was cut from a stale cached
   origin/main; the flip step was unexecutable as written and the executor
   stopped and surfaced per Core 11 rather than merging or hand-editing.
   The two sound commits merged; the flip re-issued as bin-land-flip with a
   fetch-and-verify-base step, and completed cleanly.
5. skills/directive-authoring.md changed mid-session (tree-disposition rule
   replacing the exclusive-trees bullet); the read-before-every-authoring rule
   caught the change on the next directive.
6. Every landed branch was verified by commit stats over the connector before
   merging; every merge was a merge commit; no verification found a
   discrepancy across seven merges.
7. bin/flip-agreed ran clean (exit 0, empty output) with the pointer in the
   exact form the cycle-7 artifact's Reviewed line states; the flip commit was
   +2/−2 frontmatter-only.
8. Directive length grew across the session as lore additions accumulated
   (three executor-observed additions folded in by session end).

## Interpretation

- (1, 2) Prescribing exact fix wording in directives moved gaps rather than
  closing them, twice; and even a reviewer's own quoted Fix text can exceed
  its stated scope. Pointer-to-Fix-field transfers the wording risk to the
  reviewed record, where discrepancies get surfaced (2) instead of silently
  authored.
- (3) The no-retrofit rule worked as designed: a false *observed* claim in a
  review artifact was caught by the next cycle's independent read, and the
  record of the error is itself now on the record.
- (4) The stale-worktree incident is the same failure class the checkout
  guard exists for, reintroduced by a new mechanism (worktree add resolving a
  cached ref). Environment lore that names a mechanism rather than the
  underlying invariant ("base equals the directive's cited SHA") re-admits
  the failure through the next new mechanism.
- (5) Re-reading governed skills before every authoring act is cheap and paid
  off concretely once this session.
- (6, 7) Stats-verification before merge and tool-mediated flips cost little
  and produced zero incidents; the one incident occurred in the one step
  where mechanics were hand-specified in prose (worktree creation).

## Durable insights

- Carry reviewer Fix fields as pointers; state intent, not wording, for
  everything else the executor drafts. Verbatim prescription by a
  non-reviewer author has a measured two-for-two moved-gap record this
  session.
- Directive preconditions should pin invariants, not command sequences:
  "confirm origin/main resolves to <cited base SHA> before branching"
  survives mechanism changes that "checkout then branch" does not.
- The finding-per-cycle tail converged (7 cycles to agreed for a
  dictated-origin PRD with an active reviewer). The per-cycle cost (~6–7 min
  executor time) was low enough that fixing every non-blocking finding until
  a genuinely judgment-level one appeared (cycle-7 F1) was the right default.
- bin/land's PRD journey validated its own premise: the lore block this
  session had to carry in every directive is the prose the tool exists to
  delete.

## Candidate methodology changes

- skills/directive-authoring.md: add the invariant-over-mechanism rule for
  preconditions (pin the expected base SHA; a creation command alone is not a
  precondition). Input to a future cycle, not a decision.
- The fetch-before-worktree-add and no-push-u lore belong in the write-path
  usage document (or bin/land itself) when authored; the OPEN-ITEMS gate
  entry is the tracking hook.
