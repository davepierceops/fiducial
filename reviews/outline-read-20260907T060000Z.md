## OU-02 — blocking
Claim: The two rules stating what the Copy Editor and the Critic file against the claims list were cut, and nothing in the new text or in R1164 replaces them.
Location: process/outline.md:29-31 — the surviving sentence "The Copy Editor's claims-tier audit and the Critic's Skeptic pass read the finished prose against this list."; baseline process/outline.md @ 7549f7e, "## 3. Claims list"

The document cites only R1164, which states nothing about findings, and no row under rules/ was found stating either rule — the store has no `outline` topic at the base ref.

Fix: Restore both clauses onto the surviving sentence — "...read the finished prose against this list: a claim the prose makes that is absent here is a finding, and a claim here the prose drops is a finding." They are one sentence and they name the acts of two roles the document already names.
