## O2 — observation

Location: specs/bin-land.md §6 AC-LAND-01a (lines 280-281); §3 J1; §4 G1, first arm.
Evidence: Inferred by reading, with the induced state verified by running. The document says nothing about detached HEAD or worktrees anywhere — `grep -ni 'detached\|worktree' specs/bin-land.md` returns nothing at this SHA (*observed*) — so the coverage change is implicit in the wording rather than stated.
Consequence: Nothing goes wrong, which is why this is an observation and not a finding. The widening is in the direction §6 wants: G1's first arm states no condition on the local tree at all, so any narrowing of 01a's given below that is a gap, and this removes one that had stood since cycle 2 unnoticed by four reviews.
