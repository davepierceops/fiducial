## On what the revision got right, and the shape of what it did not

Cycle 5 sent this document back with two blocking findings pulling in opposite
directions — M8 weaker than its source in one clause, stricter in another. Both
are closed, and closed the right way. B2's fix is the one worth naming: rather
than a carve-out in AC-DT-13, the time-component rule was closed **at the
source**, by a third expedited amendment to `skills/directive-authoring.md`, and
the document now says so in the same paragraph as the rule. That is the third
time this effort has taken that route and the first time the document states it
as the pattern rather than as an incident. B1's scoping is the honest version of
what M8 can decide: the cycle-5 sentence claiming the pattern is "determined by
the generator's mode" was deleted rather than qualified, the replacement says
plainly that no filename check can decide mode-appropriateness, and the residual
is written down as an accepted defect class instead of being argued away.

What sends this to `changes-required` is that the two dispositions that closed
cycle 5's findings each opened a clause-sized gap of the same family. N1 put a
character class into M8 that no governed file states — which is cycle 5's B2 in
miniature, arriving in the same revision that closed cycle 5's B2, and colliding
with the preserved contract in the way §4's own conflict rule was written to
catch. B1 named a property the lint cannot check and did not add it to the
criterion whose whole job is enumerating properties the lint does not check.
Neither is a departure from what was directed; both are consequences of executing
what was directed, which is the more useful thing for this gate to have found.
The pattern across cycles 3 through 6 is now legible: each cycle closes the
finding it was given and leaves one clause behind, and each clause has been
smaller than the last. That is convergence, but it is convergence that has needed
a gate every time to see the leftover — which is the argument for the independent
review-only session the cycle-6 directive schedules next.
