## B3 — blocking
Claim: §5's third outcome reads a count of **contradiction instances** against a baseline stated in **directive files**, so the comparison it specifies produces the wrong conclusion rather than no conclusion.
Location: specs/directive-tooling.md §5, third outcome, `Baseline:` and `Mechanism:` clauses (`:874-881`) and the cycle-7 narrowing paragraph (`:898-916`).
Evidence: Verified by reading, against sources read whole. The baseline reads "the merge-mechanism contradiction across eight directives and their successor" — grammatically **one** contradiction, instantiated across **nine** files. The mechanism reads "a recount of **contradiction instances** over directives authored after adoption, read against that baseline of nine," and the narrowing paragraph inherits the phrase unchanged ("counted against the nine-directive merge-mechanism baseline"). The source resolves the unit and does not support nine-as-instances: `docs/research/gh-write-friction-20260823T184149Z.md` §3.2 (`:98-103`) describes one disagreement — eight directives instructing squash-merge via authenticated `curl`, one (`pass2-held-fix-20260823T180753Z.md`) instructing its opposite — and the document elsewhere calls it "the eight-versus-one contradiction" (`:222`) and "The two contradictory committed directives (§3.2)" (`:351`). So the instance-unit baseline is 1 (one contradiction) or 2 (two opposed positions); it is not 9 under any reading the source offers. Cycle 7's N1 identified this and filed it non-blocking; its second limb — that the post-adoption set is empty at adoption, so the first recount returns 0 regardless of whether the tools work — I also confirm, and it is the part that is a genuine design question rather than a correctable number.
Consequence: A reader evaluating this outcome counts, say, 2 contradiction instances among post-adoption directives, compares against the stated baseline of 9, and records an improvement. The true instance baseline is 1, so 2 instances is a **regression**. Following §5 as written yields the opposite of the correct conclusion — not an unfalsifiable outcome but a reliably misleading one, which is worse. §5 is a required PRD section and the Spec Reviewer's PRD checklist requires it to name the signal, the baseline where known, and the mechanism by which it is observed; the first and third are clean and the second is in the wrong unit. This has now been raised in three consecutive cycles (5's O4, 6's N2, 7's N1) and three revisions have re-worded around it without changing the unit, which is evidence the severity has been mis-set rather than that the defect is minor.
Fix: State the unit once and make the baseline agree with it. If the unit is contradiction instances, the baseline is 1 (or 2, if opposed positions are the unit) and the nine-file figure becomes context, cited as the spread of the single contradiction across the corpus. If the unit is files carrying a contradicted instruction, the baseline is 9 and the mechanism must say it recounts files, not instances. Separately, address the empty-set limb cycle 7 raised: either state that this outcome is not evaluable until N post-adoption directives exist, or express it as a rate rather than a count, so an early 0 is not read as success.

## N1 — non-blocking
Claim: AC-DT-02 as written is not implementable — a literal reading fails for every generator, because governed source files contain blank and trivially short lines that any string literal reproduces.
Location: specs/directive-tooling.md §6 AC-DT-02 (`:952-958`); G11 (`:393-416`).
Evidence: Inferred by reading, on observed sources. AC-DT-02 requires that "no string literal in the generator's source reproduces **a line** of that entry's committed content." G11 defines a manifest entry as carrying "the committed path that region was read from," so an entry's committed content is a whole governed file. Every governed file in the companion set contains blank lines, and several contain lines as short as `- ` or `` — while any Python generator's source contains literals such as `""`, `"\n"`, and `"- "`. Under the stated reading the criterion fails universally and cannot be satisfied by a correct implementation. The criterion also carries no minimum length, no non-triviality rule, and no restriction to lines that are distinctive of the source.
Consequence: An implementer writing the static check either implements the criterion as stated and gets a permanently red gate, or invents an unstated threshold — a minimum line length, a whitespace-stripping rule, a uniqueness test — and the criterion no longer means the same thing in two implementations. Since AC-DT-02 is the mechanical enforcement of G1, which §7 lists under "Not accepted," the criterion that guards the document's central prohibition is the one that cannot be run as written.
Fix: Bound what counts as a reproduced line — a non-blank line above a stated minimum length, or a line that occurs in the cited source and not in the generator's own scaffolding — and state the bound in the criterion. The alternative framing, which may be cleaner, is to assert the property over contiguous runs: no string literal reproduces N or more consecutive non-blank lines of an entry's committed content.
