## B1 — blocking
Claim: A generator conforming to AC-DT-03 emits a skeleton from which a faithful author cannot learn the label form M3 matches, and AC-DT-03 and AC-DT-06's fixture (v) contradict each other on whether the generator may supply it.
Location: specs/directive-tooling.md:1633–1658 (AC-DT-03, both regions and the verification clause); :1750–:1753 (AC-DT-06 fixture (v)); :578–:596 (§4 G3, "The disposition prompt"); :750 (§4, M3's row, "the label's fixed **lexical form** is a **TRD decision**").
Evidence: **Verified by reading**, at the pinned commit, and by running for the enumeration of sites. M3 matches labelled disposition statements, and the label's fixed lexical form is delegated to the TRD (`:750`, *observed*). AC-DT-03 fixes the prompt region's content **by enumeration** — "it is read from committed text (G1), it states that a working-tree disposition is required, it names both admitted forms, and it states that the disposition is written as its own **labelled statement**" (`:1636-1641`) — and the label's lexical form is not in that list. It then fixes the author region as "present and **empty**" (`:1645`) and states, in its own words, that "an empty author region leaves the file carrying **no labelled disposition statement**" (`:1650-1653`), with the verification clause asserting the region "is present and blank" (`:1658`). AC-DT-06's fixture (v) contemplates the opposite: "or, **if the TRD's label form is emitted with the empty slot**, one carrying neither admitted form" (`:1751-1752`). `grep -n "lexical form\|label's fixed\|label form\|labelled statement"` over the whole document returns no site requiring the generator, the prompt, or any manifest-named committed source to carry the label's lexical form (*observed*, by running). §8 carries no open question for it either (N2). Neither binary exists, so no skeleton was generated; this is a reading of the criteria.
Consequence: Concrete, and on the main journey rather than an edge. A decision session runs `bin/directive`, reads a prompt that tells it to write "its own labelled statement" and does not say what a label is, writes the disposition into the empty author region with a label of its own devising, and hands the directive over. At the executor's first act (J2) M3 finds zero labelled statements of the TRD-fixed form and exits non-zero, so J1's own output fails J2 — the generator guiding an author into a directive the same binary's lint fails, which is exactly the defect cycle 15's O4 records this document's author-session catching at `d0230b8a` one level up and which is unclosed one level down. Two implementations, both conforming to §6, differing in what the file contains — the shape cycle 8's B2 and cycle 13's B1 were each blocking for. Note what is **not** wrong here: nothing about M3's contract, and nothing about the prompt naming the labelling requirement, which is correct and well-sourced. What is missing is any statement of the property that would make the two halves agree — no goal or criterion says that a skeleton's faithful completion passes the lint.
Fix: Two options; the choice is Dave's and neither is taken here, though the first is the one this reviewer would propose, **clearly labelled as a proposal**. **(a) Route the label form through the invariant-text source.** Add the label's lexical form to AC-DT-03's enumeration of what the prompt states, and require it to be read from the committed source the manifest names — which makes Q1's answer the home for it, keeps G1 intact, and makes M3's requirement governed text rather than a TRD-private constant (which also closes part of N1's G6 exposure). AC-DT-06(v)'s conditional clause then goes, the author region staying blank. **(b) Emit the label with the slot.** Require the generator to emit the label into the author region and restate AC-DT-03's "present and empty / blank" as "present, carrying the label and no disposition text, structurally distinguishable from a filled region", and AC-DT-06(v) as unconditional. Either way, one of AC-DT-03 and AC-DT-06(v) must move, and this document should carry, as a goal or as a clause of G3, the property both options exist to secure: **a skeleton whose author region is filled faithfully passes M3**.
