## F-2 — non-blocking
Claim: The new second bullet bounds the label to "no other unfenced line", where the design and the shipped check bound it to eligible lines, so the governed rule forbids two shapes the mask deliberately permits.
Location: `skills/directive-authoring.md:22-23`, against `skills/directive-invariants.md:25-29` and `:222-230`
Evidence: *Observed*, verified by running. The bullet reads "**The disposition label leads the disposition statement and no other unfenced line.**" The invariants document's Disposition label section defines eligibility at `:222-230`: a line is eligible unless masked, and fenced blocks, **blockquote lines**, and **HTML comments** all mask. `bin/aimeta/mdmask.py:36-71` implements exactly that, and the shipped `unfenced_labelled_statements` scans only eligible indices. So "unfenced" is a strictly wider set than "eligible": a labelled line inside a blockquote or an HTML comment is unfenced and not eligible. `specs/directive-tooling-trd.md` §3.5 states why both are masked — for blockquotes, "carriage of another document's wording is the common case for both" — and §3.4 discloses the masking as narrowing what the lint matches rather than adding a requirement it enforces. The direction is the same one cycle 4's O-3 recorded against the invariants document's own paragraph; the consolidation corrected that paragraph to "eligible" (`skills/directive-invariants.md:25`) in the same commit that wrote "unfenced" into this bullet.
Consequence: A directive that carries another directive's disposition in a blockquote — the case §3.5 names as the common one, and the shape a gate or amendment directive quoting its predecessor produces — passes M3 and violates this bullet as written. The failure is silent at authoring time and asymmetric at review time: the lint is green, so nothing stops the directive, but a reviewer citing the governing skill can call a lint-passing directive non-conformant, and an author following the skill avoids a shape the design permits. The same holds for the commented-out placeholder block cycle mode emits, whose shape `bin/cycle-open`'s skeleton already established as non-content.
Fix: State the bound in the term the design and the check both use — "the disposition label leads the disposition statement and no other eligible line" — and let the eligibility definition stay where it is, in the invariants document the previous bullet already names as the holder. One word, no bullet moves, and it puts this document and the one it was revised alongside on the same bound.
