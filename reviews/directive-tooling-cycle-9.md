## B1 — blocking
Claim: The **working-tree disposition slot is classified two incompatible ways** — G1 lists it among the invariant sections read from committed text, G3/J1/AC-DT-03 have the author fill it — and G11's manifest requires every region to be one or the other, so §5's first measurement and AC-DT-05's partition are under-determined at the one region where the two classes overlap.
Location: specs/directive-tooling.md §4 G1; §4 G3; §4 G11; §3 J1; §5, first outcome ("Attribution mechanism"); §6 AC-DT-03, AC-DT-05, AC-DT-18.
Evidence: Verified by reading, against the reviewed text. G1: "Every invariant section the skeleton carries — sandbox constraints, stop conditions, **working-tree disposition slot**, verification steps, report format, claim labels — is read from committed repo text at generation time." G11: the manifest names every region "each entry carrying **either** the committed path that region was read from **or** an explicit marking that the region is the author's slot," and "the generator-supplied share is the sum of the extents whose entries name a committed path." J1 has the author "fills the task-specific middle **and the disposition slot**"; AC-DT-03 requires the slot to be "present and empty" and "distinguishable from a slot that has been filled." So the slot is simultaneously (a) an invariant section read from committed text, per G1, and (b) a region the author writes into, per J1 and AC-DT-03 — and G11 admits exactly one author's slot, singular, which J1 identifies as the task-specific middle. G11's own justification for marking the author slot applies verbatim to the disposition slot and is not applied to it: "without it an invariant section's extent would run through whatever the author wrote after it, and the share would compute to everything."
Consequence: Concretely, for a landed directive of 60 lines whose author wrote a four-line exclusive assignment into the disposition slot, the manifest entry for that region names a committed path (G1), so those four author-written lines are summed into "the share the generator supplied" and §5's first signal reports ~7% more generator share than the file has. The signal §5 names is therefore biased upward by exactly the bytes of the element this whole spec exists to make visible — the motivating incident's element. Separately, AC-DT-05 claims the partition is "computable from the committed artifact alone" and yields one number; two implementers, one marking the slot per G1 and one per J1, compute two different numbers for the same file, so the criterion does not pin the behaviour it is written to pin. This is a defect in the document, not in a future implementation: the Spec Reviewer's PRD checklist requires §5 to name "the mechanism by which it is observed," and this mechanism has two answers.
Fix: Decide the slot's representation in the manifest and state it once, at G11, with G1 and AC-DT-05 made to agree. Two dispositions are available and the document does not choose between them here. **(a)** The slot is **two regions** — a generator-supplied prompt naming the two admitted forms, read from `skills/directive-authoring.md` per G1, followed by an empty **author region** — in which case G11 admits two author markings, AC-DT-18's "exactly one task-specific slot" stands unchanged, and the share computes correctly. **(b)** The slot is **one author region**, in which case G1's enumeration drops it and the prompt text it carries is stated as generator-supplied within an author-marked region, which requires G11 to say what an author-marked region's generator-supplied preamble is and how the share treats it. (a) is the smaller change and the one AC-DT-05's partition already supports. Either way AC-DT-18's second bound — which records this as open rather than answering it — is removed once the answer lands.
Related: N1.

## N1 — non-blocking
Claim: AC-DT-02's new threshold makes the criterion **decidable** but not **satisfiable**: a non-blank line short enough to be structural still matches exactly after whitespace normalization, and at least one such line is present in every governed source the manifest will name.
Location: specs/directive-tooling.md §6 AC-DT-02; §4 G1, G11.
Evidence: Verified by running. `grep -c '^---$'` returns **2** for each of `docs/global-context/core.md`, `docs/global-context/decision-layer.md`, and `skills/directive-authoring.md` — the frontmatter fences — and each is a non-blank line of those files' committed content. Under AC-DT-02 as revised, a generator holding the string literal `"---"`, for any purpose (emitting frontmatter into the skeleton, a horizontal rule, a fence), reproduces a non-blank line of every one of those entries exactly after normalization, and the static check reports it. The same shape holds for any short structural line: `bin/cycle-open`'s own `render_directive` (`:115-133`) holds the literals `"## Decisions"`, `"## Deferred / out of scope"`, and `"## Execution notes"`, and the criterion offers no rule that would distinguish a structural marker from invariant prose. Cycle 8's N1 offered two fixes — "a non-blank line **above a stated minimum length**" and the contiguous-run framing — and the dictated disposition took non-blankness and the comparison rule and neither bound.
Consequence: An implementer building AC-DT-02 as now written gets a red static check for a generator that is correct — the failure shape cycle 8's N1 named, narrowed from "every implementation" to "any implementation that emits a structural markdown line," which is most of them. The criterion is the mechanical enforcement of G1, and G1 is the goal §7 lists under "Not accepted," so the guard on the document's central prohibition is the one that reds spuriously. Non-blocking rather than blocking because the criterion is now **decidable** — two implementations agree on what it reports, which is what cycle 8's N1 asked for and what the disposition delivered — and because the residual is a false-positive class an implementer can surface at the TRD stage rather than a property no implementation can satisfy.
Fix: Add the non-triviality bound the comparison rule needs: either a minimum normalized length below which a line is not a reproduction, or cycle 8's alternative framing — no literal reproduces **N or more consecutive** non-blank lines of an entry's committed content — which subsumes the length question and is closer to what G1 is protecting.
