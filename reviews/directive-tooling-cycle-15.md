## N1 — non-blocking
Claim: M3's requirement that **exactly one** labelled disposition statement be present carries an **at-most-one** half that the governed rule does not state as a count rule, and that half is the only reason §7's second residual exists; AC-DT-13 and §7's "Not accepted" both assert that no element enforces a requirement no governed file states.
Location: specs/directive-tooling.md §4 M3 row (`specs/directive-tooling.md:750`); §6 AC-DT-06's M3 fixture list; §7, "Residual two — a labelled statement under quotation"; §6 AC-DT-13; §7, "Not accepted".
Evidence: **Verified by reading**, against the governed source at the cited commit. The rule at `skills/directive-authoring.md` @ `b4a0fa58` reads, whole: "Every directive states its working-tree disposition — either an exclusive assignment (a named directory plus the command creating it) or an explicit sole-tree declaration. A prohibition is not a disposition. The disposition is stated as its own labelled statement, mechanically distinguishable from incidental mention of trees or commands elsewhere in the file; the label's fixed form is a tooling concern, not this document's." (*observed*, matched against the blob by running.) Three of M3's four clauses read straight off that sentence: **at least one** ("Every directive states"), **form-membership** and its exclusivity ("either … or"), and **label-presence** ("its own labelled statement"). The fourth — that **two or more** labelled statements fail — is carried only by the definite singular, "its working-tree disposition" and "The disposition". The sentence states no count. The cycle-15 directive dictates the clause ("exactly one labelled disposition statement is present"), so the document is executing an instruction; but a dictated requirement is not thereby a **governed** one, and G6 is about the latter.
Consequence: Two, and the second is the one that bites. First, AC-DT-13 says "Every element M1–M8 satisfies this as written" and §7's "Not accepted" says "**Three** elements have stood in that position in this effort and **none does now**"; if the at-most-one clause is unsourced, both sentences are false at this revision, in the same shape cycle 5's N1 recorded for M8's timestamp rule and cycle 6's B2 closed by amending the source. Second, and independently of how that is resolved: the at-most-one clause is what creates §7's **residual two**. A directive that quotes another directive's labelled disposition statement — a provenance note, a carried constraint, a citation of a prior cycle, all of which this effort's own directives do routinely — carries two labelled statements and exits non-zero while stating exactly one disposition of its own. Drop the clause and that residual has no mechanism; keep it and the document accepts a false positive whose frequency it cannot bound, because it also does not say whether M3's match is sensitive to markdown context. A labelled statement inside a fenced block or a blockquote is the ordinary way a directive carries a quotation, and nothing in §4 or §6 says whether M3 sees it. That is a TRD-stage question the PRD leaves unstated, and it changes the residual's size by an order of magnitude in either direction.
Fix: **Clearly labelled as a proposal; the choice is Dave's and none is taken here.** **(a) Narrow M3 to at-least-one.** M3 becomes: at least one labelled disposition statement is present, and each carries exactly one admitted form. The at-most-one clause goes, §7's residual two goes with it, and AC-DT-13's and §7's claims become true without an amendment. The cost is that a directive stating two conflicting dispositions passes M3, which no other element catches. **(b) Keep exactly-one and source it.** An expedited amendment to `skills/directive-authoring.md` stating that a directive states **one** working-tree disposition would close the G6 exposure by the mechanism this effort has used three times, and would leave residual two standing and accepted. **(c) Keep exactly-one and record the exposure.** Add the clause to AC-DT-13 and §7's "Not accepted" as a fourth element standing in that position, which makes the acceptance auditable but leaves both sentences carrying an exception where they currently carry none. Whichever is taken, the markdown-context question should be stated at M3's row or routed to §8, because residual two cannot be sized without it.
