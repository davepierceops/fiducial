## Reconciliation against the digest

The digest's 1043 surviving distinct rules is a corpus-wide figure that dedupes
across files. Ruling 1 forbids that deduplication in the store — a cross-file
cluster-mate belongs to its own file's topic and is written there — so the
store's row count is not that number and was never going to be. The comparable
figure is the digest's per-topic distinct counts, which sum to 1257 after retirement,
less the eleven engagement cards' 141, giving 1116; against that, 973 is 143
lower. The gap is accounted for by, in order of size:

- the 208 retirements, of which 124 were forecast by DEC-000380 and the rest are
  ruling-4 container rules, ruling-5 bare negations, and the C004 scope
  statements Dave's ruling turns into the `session` key;
- Dave's own ruled merges (C002, C017, C024, C025, C097, C125, C153, C206) and
  the ruled merges in the core and decision-layer row lists;
- rulings 2 and 5, which collapse restatements and negations the clusters
  artifact never paired, and which no per-file digest figure could anticipate.
