TASK

Rewrite ten process documents so each carries only what an agent acts on.
The human read the first bundle and found the process documents still full
of prose, persuasion, organization, and record-keeping. Apply the row
criteria to prose. One commit per document, in the order below; the
document keeps its path and its frontmatter shape; the rest is yours to
cut.

THE CRITERIA, per sentence:

1. Keep a sentence that tells an agent what to do, in what order, or under
   what condition. Keep a form (a template's fields, a schema's header, an
   entry's layout) verbatim where the document is a form.
2. Keep an explanation only where an agent needs the reasoning to act
   correctly — to choose between cases, or to recognize the condition. "Why
   this rule exists" is not that; cut it.
3. Cut persuasion: every sentence arguing that the document is right, that
   the alternative was worse, that a reader should be convinced.
4. Cut the document's record of itself: agreement history, review
   pointers, what it changed or retired, what it supersedes, register ids
   in the body. That record lives in the decision log and the fix logs.
5. Cut organization that exists for a human reader: introductions,
   summaries, "this section governs", restatements of the document's own
   structure. Headings stay where they separate steps or forms; a heading
   over one paragraph goes.
6. Negation only where no positive form exists. "Do not X" becomes "Do Y"
   where Y is the act; where the rule is a ban with no positive act, keep
   it and name the incident it prevents in one clause, not a paragraph.
7. A sentence that restates a row is cut and the row is cited by id in its
   place, in brackets: "[R0486]". Every id cited must exist under rules/.
8. Each sentence that survives says one thing. A merged sentence states it
   at its shortest.
9. The frontmatter's `role` list is decided per document: the roles that
   perform an act the document describes, not every role that might read
   it. A document keyed to all twelve roles is a defect (DEC-000470's
   reasoning). State the reasoning for each document's list in the report.
10. "Dave" does not appear; "the human" does (ruled 2026-09-06). Leave
    pronouns as they are; the human rewrites those rows himself.
