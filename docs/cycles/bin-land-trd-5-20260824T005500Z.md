## Standing decision — the governing principle for this cycle

Emission scope has three homes in specs/bin-land-trd.md: §3.7's per-step
returns, §5.3's key table with its "Emitted on" column, and §6's eleven
failure-mode cells. They disagree, and four cycles of pairwise fixes have each
exposed the next disagreement rather than closed it (O1). This cycle collapses
the three homes to one.

**§5.3's key table is the single home.** For every contract field and detail
key, the table states — exhaustively and in one place — what it is and on which
paths it appears. Nothing else in the document makes an independent claim about
when a key appears.

This supersedes D-O2's construction of §6 (cells as independently authored
exhaustive statements) while preserving D-O2's intent (each cell exhaustive).
It preserves D-O1's floor rule as the table's one absence rule. Stated inline
because this directive is the decision's origin.
